# Power BI — 7 Page Executive Report

## Page 1 — Executive Growth Quality
KPI cards: ARR, MRR, NRR, GRR, Active Logos, ARPA, At-Risk ARR.
Charts: ARR trend, NRR/GRR trend, segment scorecard, top 5 quantified issues.

## Page 2 — ARR Bridge & Retention
Waterfall: Opening MRR → New → Expansion → Contraction → Churn → Closing.
Cohort heatmap: logo retention by signup cohort and account age.
Cuts: segment, region, billing term.

## Page 3 — Customer Health & Renewals
Health-band distribution, ARR at risk, 90-day renewal table, account drill-through.

## Page 4 — Pricing & Monetization
Discount distribution, excess-discount leakage, leakage by segment/plan/account.

## Page 5 — Product Adoption & Expansion
Seat utilization, product attach, high-utilization accounts, expansion candidate table.

## Page 6 — Acquisition Economics
New logos, spend, CAC, channel mix and retained-quality comparison.

## Page 7 — Customer Success Operating Model
CSM portfolio ARR, risk ARR, portfolio capacity utilization, recommended rebalance.

Use a Date dimension and drill-through to an Account 360 page if desired.
