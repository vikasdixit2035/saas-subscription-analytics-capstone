# Executive Story — Instructor Reference

## Executive answer
Northstar Cloud has approximately **£1092.6m active ARR** across **4,328 active customer accounts**. Growth is meaningful, but the installed base still leaks value through gross retention, low-adoption risk and commercial discounting.

## Reference evidence
- Latest NRR: **97.8%**
- Latest GRR: **95.6%**
- Critical / At-Risk active ARR: **£47.1m**
- Annualized excess-discount leakage: **£20.90m**
- Healthy + ≥80% utilization ARR pool: **£166.9m**
- Most common primary churn reason: **Low Adoption**

## 90-day management agenda
1. Retention command center: rank renewals by ARR, health and timing.
2. Adoption rescue: intervene on low-utilization accounts before renewal.
3. Discount governance: review accounts above plan-standard discount.
4. Targeted expansion: focus on healthy, highly utilized accounts and product whitespace.
5. CSM portfolio redesign: allocate coverage using risk-adjusted ARR, not account count.
