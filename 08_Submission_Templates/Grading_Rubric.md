# Grading Rubric — 100 Points
- Data quality and reconciliation: 10
- SQL correctness and readability: 20
- Python EDA / cohorts / health scoring: 20
- Power BI semantic model and executive report: 25
- Executive recommendation memo: 15
- Technical documentation: 5
- Consulting-style story and prioritization: 5

## Distinction standard
The learner reconciles metrics across tools, distinguishes addressable pools from forecast impact, explains assumptions, and makes a small number of quantified management recommendations.
