# What Is Unique About This Capstone

This project moves beyond a normal churn dashboard.

1. **Recurring-revenue accounting:** learners construct an MRR/ARR bridge and distinguish new, expansion, contraction and churn.
2. **NRR vs GRR:** they learn why logo churn alone is insufficient for SaaS management.
3. **Cohort retention:** they compare customers by acquisition vintage and account age, rather than mixing new and mature customers.
4. **Customer-health scoring:** usage, NPS, support friction, payments and revenue trend are combined into an interpretable risk score.
5. **Pricing leakage:** actual discounting is compared against plan-standard discounts and converted into annual financial leakage.
6. **Expansion analytics:** the solution finds healthy, highly utilized accounts that are credible upsell candidates.
7. **Acquisition economics:** CAC is evaluated alongside retention quality; cheap acquisition is not automatically good acquisition.
8. **Customer-success capacity:** CSM portfolios are compared using ARR, risk ARR and capacity instead of account count alone.
9. **Opportunity sizing:** every major insight is translated into an addressable financial pool and capture-rate scenarios.
10. **Consulting workflow:** learners use a problem statement, issue tree, hypothesis register, KPI tree, analysis workplan and executive storyline.
11. **Data-quality gate:** deliberately imperfect raw data must be reconciled before analysis.
12. **Executive communication:** the final deliverable is an answer-first recommendation memo rather than a collection of visuals.
