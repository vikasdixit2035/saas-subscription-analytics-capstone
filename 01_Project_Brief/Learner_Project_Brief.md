# Northstar Cloud — SaaS Subscription Analytics Capstone

## Your role
You are a Data Analyst on a consulting engagement supporting the CEO, CFO, CRO and Chief Customer Officer of Northstar Cloud.

## Executive problem
ARR is growing, but management does not know whether growth is durable. They want to answer:
- Where is recurring revenue leaking?
- Which customers are at highest retention risk?
- Which customers should be prioritized for expansion?
- Are discounts creating avoidable leakage?
- Which acquisition channels create durable ARR?
- Is Customer Success capacity allocated to the right accounts?

## Required deliverables
1. Data-quality issue log and cleaned analytical layer.
2. PostgreSQL database and 50 SQL answers.
3. Python EDA notebook with cohort retention, customer health and opportunity sizing.
4. Power BI report with 7 pages.
5. Executive recommendation memo with quantified actions.
6. Supporting account-level priority lists.

## Mandatory executive KPIs
MRR, ARR, Active Logos, ARPA, Logo Retention, NRR, GRR, Expansion MRR, Contraction MRR, Churned MRR, seat utilization, NPS, product attach rate, CAC, discount leakage, ARR at risk.

## Engagement phases
### Phase 1 — Frame
Create a problem statement, issue tree and hypotheses before opening Power BI.

### Phase 2 — Validate
Identify duplicates, missing values, inconsistent categories, invalid NPS and malformed percentages.

### Phase 3 — Build the metric layer
Create auditable recurring-revenue measures. Do not calculate NRR independently in every chart.

### Phase 4 — Diagnose
Use segment, industry, region, channel, plan, billing term, adoption, support and payment behaviour to identify drivers.

### Phase 5 — Size opportunities
Calculate risk ARR, expansion pool, pricing recovery and renewal pool. Apply realistic capture-rate scenarios.

### Phase 6 — Recommend
Produce no more than five management actions. Every action must have an owner, KPI, 90-day step and quantified impact range.

## Submission standard
The work should answer “So what?” and “What should management do next?” The dashboard is evidence; it is not the final answer.
